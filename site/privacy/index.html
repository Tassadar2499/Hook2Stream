Hosted service policy

Privacy Policy

Effective September 4, 2026

Hook2Stream is an open-source project with an invite-only hosted MVP. This policy explains the hosted service's limited use of account data and creative material. It does not govern independently operated, self-hosted copies of the software.

Who operates the service

The Hook2Stream project maintainer operates the hosted MVP and determines how data is used for that service. No company, office address or compliance certification is represented by this policy. For privacy questions or requests, email markevich.roma@gmail.com.

Data we collect

  • Google sign-in data. With your permission, we request only the openid, email and profile scopes. We keep your Google subject identifier, verified email address and, if available, display name to authenticate you, check the invite list and show your account. The Google access token used during sign-in is not retained by Hook2Stream.
  • Creative content. We receive the songs, file names, release details, rights confirmations, transcripts, artwork, edits and campaign choices that you provide, plus generated previews, videos and export bundles.
  • Service records. We keep workspace and project metadata, job state, consent versions, entitlements, audit events and security session data in PostgreSQL so the workflow can run reliably and access can be enforced.
  • Technical data. Server logs may contain an IP address, browser or request metadata, timestamps, response status and diagnostic identifiers. Sensitive upload and download routes are excluded from access logs; cookies, authorization headers and query strings are removed or redacted.
  • Messages. If you email the maintainer, we receive your address and whatever you include in the message.

How we use data

We use this data only to provide and secure sign-in, run the requested media and AI workflow, preserve your edits, generate and deliver release assets, troubleshoot failures, prevent abuse, maintain backups and comply with valid legal obligations. We do not sell personal data, serve advertising or use your content to train our own models.

Service providers and disclosures

Data is shared only as needed to operate the service:

  • Google handles OAuth sign-in and returns the basic identity data described above.
  • OpenRouter and routed model providers process the audio, text, release context or image instructions needed for AI features. Hook2Stream is configured to require Zero Data Retention routing and to disallow prompt collection. OpenRouter still records request metadata, and provider processing remains subject to their own terms and policies.
  • Storj stores private media objects after Hook2Stream encrypts them with H2SE. PostgreSQL backups are encrypted with age before upload. Storj and its gateway process ciphertext and operational object-access metadata.
  • Infrastructure providers supply the VPS, networking, DNS, source hosting and deployment systems needed to operate Hook2Stream.

We may also disclose information when required by law, or when reasonably necessary to protect users, the service or others. The hosted service currently has no ads or data-broker sharing.

Payments

Production billing is disabled as of the effective date. Hook2Stream does not currently send production payment details to Stripe, process charges or offer paid checkout. A private staging environment may use Stripe's test mode with test-only data. This policy will be updated before live billing is introduced.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept only for the workflow, security and recovery periods configured for the MVP. Login state expires after about 10 minutes and an authenticated session after no more than 7 days. Incomplete uploads and temporary processing objects are normally eligible for cleanup after 24 hours. Inactive projects without a paid entitlement are normally eligible for cleanup after 30 days.

Deleting a project starts a fenced cleanup process scheduled to purge its live media within 7 days. Limited project, audit or deletion records may remain where needed to preserve system integrity or meet legal obligations. Encrypted backups expire on a separate schedule—up to 7 days for staging and 35 days for production—so deleted data may remain in encrypted backups until those backups expire. Backup contents are decrypted only when needed for disaster recovery.

You can delete a project in the app. To request access, correction or deletion of account-level data, email the contact address above. We may verify the request before acting. You can also revoke Hook2Stream's Google access from your Google Account.

Security and international processing

Hook2Stream uses HTTPS, restricted service credentials, encrypted host storage, application-layer authenticated encryption for media and encrypted off-host backups. No Internet service can promise absolute security. Providers, including global object storage and AI providers, may process data outside your country. Their locations and safeguards are governed by their own service terms and policies.

Changes

Material changes will be posted here with a new effective date. A future feature that materially changes how Google user data is used will not be enabled until any separate notice or renewed-consent step required for that feature can be provided by the MVP.
